Rinjani Join Group Trek Prices

Two simple ways to join a sharing group on Mount Rinjani: Trek Only if you get to the village yourself, or Standard with transport and hotel.

Good news for motorbike riders 🛵

Already rented a motorbike? Save money on your trek.

If you have rented a motorbike in Lombok and want to climb Mount Rinjani, we have a special price for you. Imagine: the 2D1N trek normally costs USD 152 – but because you ride to Senaru or Sembalun village yourself, you pay only USD 118. That money stays in your pocket for the rest of your trip.

"Cheap price, so the service must be bad?" Stop thinking that. You get the same guide, the same porters, the same tent, sleeping bag and mattress, and the same food as guests who pay the normal package. The only thing you skip is our car and the hotel night.

Why is it so affordable?

Because every trip is shared. A guide, a team of porters and a cook cost roughly the same whether they look after two trekkers or a full group of eight (the maximum per group set by the national park, TNGR). Split across a join group, your share becomes small.

On top of that we keep only a small profit per guest. We would rather run many smooth trips with happy travellers than a few expensive ones. Read our story.
