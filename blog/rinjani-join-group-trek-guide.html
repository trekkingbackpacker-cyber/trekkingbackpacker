Rinjani Join Group Trek: The Complete Guide for Budget Travellers

By Lendri, former Rinjani porter & guide · 2 November 2026 · 7 min read

Mount Rinjani is one of the most beautiful volcano treks in Southeast Asia – and one of the most expensive if you book a private trip. A Rinjani join group trek (also called a sharing group or open trip) is the budget-friendly way to climb it without giving up the tents, mattress and food you would get on a private trek.

What is a Rinjani join group trek?

On a join group trek you don't hire a whole crew for yourself. You join other travellers who booked the same route and the same start date. The group shares one guide team, the porters and the cook – and so the cost per person drops a lot.

A join group on Mount Rinjani has a maximum of 8 trekkers. This is a rule of the Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR), not something we invented. Bigger bookings are split into two groups, each with its own guide.

Who joins a sharing group?

  • Solo travellers who want trekking friends instead of trekking alone
  • Backpackers and students with a tight budget
  • Couples and small groups of friends who are happy to meet new people
  • First-time trekkers who want an experienced team to look after them

Is the quality lower on a join group trek?

It shouldn't be – and with us it isn't. Every trekker gets a tent, a sleeping bag, a mattress, a toilet tent at camp, and all meals, fruit and drinking water on the trek. A licensed English-speaking guide and porters come with every group. The only real difference from a private trek is that you share the trail – and the bill – with a few other hikers.

Two prices: Trek Only or Standard

We offer two ways to join a group:

  • Trek Only – for travellers who already rented a motorbike and ride to Senaru or Sembalun themselves. Everything on the mountain is included: 2D1N USD 118, 3D2N USD 134, 4D3N USD 143.
  • Standard sharing group – adds pick-up in Lombok, 1 night hotel in Senaru before the trek and drop-off afterwards. The price per person depends on the size of your booking.

Which join group trek should I choose?

  • 2 days, no summit: 2D1N Senaru Crater Rim
  • 2 days with the summit: 2D1N Sembalun Summit
  • The classic – summit, lake and hot springs: 3D2N Sembalun – Senaru or 3D2N Sembalun – Torean
  • A relaxed pace: 4D3N Sembalun – Senaru or 4D3N Sembalun – Torean

How to join a group

  1. Choose your trek, route and Trek Only or Standard.
  2. Send us your date and number of people on WhatsApp.
  3. We confirm your place in a join group for that date.
  4. Pay by bank transfer and get ready for Rinjani.
