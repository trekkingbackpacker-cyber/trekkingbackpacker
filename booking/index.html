Booking & payment policy

How to book and pay for your join group trek

Simple, honest rules – so you know exactly what to expect before you book your Mount Rinjani join group trek.

1. How to book

Send us a WhatsApp message with your trek, your start date, the number of people and whether you want Trek Only or Standard. We check the join group for your date and confirm your place.

2. Deposit

  • To confirm your place in a join group we ask a deposit of USD 30 per person.
  • Low season – September, October and December: no deposit needed. You pay everything when you arrive at the basecamp.

3. Payment

The rest of the payment is made at the basecamp before the trek, in cash or by bank transfer to our Indonesian bank account:

Bank transfer (Indonesia)Bank: MandiriAccount name: LENDRIAccount number: 161-00-1056679-7

Most of our guests pay in cash at the basecamp. Ask us on WhatsApp if you want to know the amount in Indonesian rupiah.

4. Bad weather

The weather on Mount Rinjani can change quickly, especially in the rainy months. As long as the national park is open, the trek goes ahead, and bad weather is not a reason for a refund. Your guide always puts safety first and may change the plan, slow down or turn the group around if the trail is not safe.

5. Sudden closure by the national park

If the Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR) suddenly closes the trails, the trek cannot go ahead – and you get your money back.

6. Need to change something?

Message us on WhatsApp as early as possible, and we will see what we can do.

  • Deposit USD 30 / person
