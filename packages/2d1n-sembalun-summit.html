This is a join group trek – here's what that means

On the 2D1N Sembalun Summit join group trek you don't trek alone with a private crew. You join a sharing group of up to 8 travellers who booked the same route and date. Maximum 8 trekkers per group is a rule of the Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR), not our own. Together you share one guide team, porters and cook, so the price per person stays low.

  • Solo travellers welcome – you trek with your new join group
  • Same facilities as a private trek: tent, thick mattress, sleeping bag, toilet tent and all meals
  • Licensed English-speaking guide and porters who carry all the camping gear
  • Trek Only if you have your own motorbike, or Standard with transport & hotel

Day-by-day join group itinerary

Times are approximate. Your guide adjusts the pace to the join group and to the weather on the mountain.

Night before the trek · Standard package

  • 15:00–18:00Pick-up within our transport area in Lombok and transfer to the hotel in Senaru.
  • EveningTrek briefing with your guide at the hotel – often you meet the rest of your join group here. We check your shoes, jacket and headlamp.
  • Trek OnlyNo hotel night: you ride your own motorbike to the village and meet your join group on the morning of Day 1.

Day 1 · Sembalun (1,156 m) → Sembalun crater rim (2,639 m)

  • 05:30Standard package: early drive from the hotel in Senaru to Sembalun (approx. 1.5 hours).
  • 07:30Trek Only guests meet the join group at the Rinjani National Park office in Sembalun. Registration for everyone.
  • 08:00Start trekking from Sembalun (1,156 m) across the open savanna towards Pos 1 and Pos 2.
  • 12:00Hot lunch cooked by the porters at Pos 2, with time to rest in the shade.
  • 13:30Continue to Pos 3, then up the steep 'Hills of Regret' (Bukit Penyesalan) – the hardest part of the day. Slow and steady.
  • 17:00Arrive at Sembalun crater rim (2,639 m). Tents are already set up. Sunset over the crater, Segara Anak lake and the summit.
  • 19:00Dinner with your join group and an early night – the summit push starts in a few hours.

Day 2 · Summit (3,726 m) → Sembalun

  • 02:00Wake-up call with hot tea and biscuits. Headlamp on, warm layers on.
  • 02:30Summit push with your guide on loose volcanic gravel. Approx. 3–4 hours, depending on the group.
  • 05:30Sunrise on the summit of Mount Rinjani (3,726 m) – views over Lombok, Bali's Mount Agung and Sumbawa on a clear day.
  • 08:00Back at the crater rim camp for a big breakfast.
  • 10:00Pack up and descend the same way back to Sembalun (approx. 5–6 hours).
  • 13:00Lunch on the way down at Pos 2 or Pos 3.
  • 16:00Arrive in Sembalun. Standard package: drop-off to Bangsal harbour, Senggigi or Mataram. Trek Only: back to your motorbike in Sembalun.

Join group questions

Is the 2D1N Sembalun Summit trek a private trip?

No. This is a join group (sharing group) trek. You join a small group of other travellers on the same date and route – a maximum of 8 trekkers per group – this is a rule of the Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR), not our own. Trekking Backpacker only runs join group treks – that is how the price stays low.

What is the difference between Trek Only and Standard?

Trek Only (USD 118 per person) is for travellers who ride their own motorbike to the village – no transport and no hotel. Standard (USD 139–154 per person, depending on group size) adds pick-up in Lombok, 1 night hotel in Senaru before the trek and drop-off afterwards.

Is the service the same on Trek Only?

Yes. Same guide, same porters, same tent, sleeping bag, mattress and toilet tent, and the same meals as on the Standard package.

Can I join the group alone?

Yes. Many solo travellers book a join group trek. You trek, eat and camp with the other people in your group.
